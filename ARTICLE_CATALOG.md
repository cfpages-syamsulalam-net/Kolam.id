# Article Catalog — Kolam.id

Scope: 108 evidence-led editorial assets across 18 parent topics for Indonesian pool and water-feature owners, designers, procurement teams, and operators. The catalog covers the complete lifecycle but does not authorize mass publication.

Domain boundaries: proposed articles are neutral education. Existing service routes own quote/purchase intent. No brief is a city/province/region swap. Kolam.co.id and other owned domains may independently cover the same subjects; only overlap inside Kolam.id is controlled here. Proposed article URLs should use a dedicated namespace such as `/panduan/<slug>/` after a live-route and CMS decision.

Catalog statistics: 18 topics; 108 briefs; exactly 6 briefs per topic; 12 first-wave assets.

Priority legend: `P0` = first bounded cluster or safety-critical foundation; `P1` = next connected authority layer; `P2` = valuable depth after foundations; `P3` = specialist/long-horizon asset needing stronger evidence or maintenance.
